About Me

Hi there! My name is Linhao Zhong (钟林昊 in Chinese). I am currently a first-year Ph.D. student at Zhejiang University, advised by Prof. Chunhua Shen. Before joining Zhejiang University, I received my Bachelor’s degree from Shanghai Jiao Tong University, where I studied in the ACM Honor Class, Zhiyuan Honors Programme.
